FAQ

Common Questions

What is the minimum order quantity? +
Our minimum order depends on the tier. Taster Pack starts at 12 units, Trade Partner at 50 units, and White Label at 150 units. We're flexible for first orders — call us to discuss.
Do you offer custom branding or white label? +
Yes! Our White Label tier includes custom branding, your logo on packaging, and bespoke gift box design. Available from 150 units. Trade Partners (50+ units) can also get custom label options.
How long does delivery take? +
Standard delivery is 3–5 business days. Gauteng orders are often next day. We also offer express turnaround for urgent orders — contact us to arrange. Free delivery on orders over R800 within Gauteng.
Can I set up a standing monthly order? +
Absolutely — standing orders are our favourite. We'll set up a monthly replenishment schedule so you never run out of stock. You'll also get priority delivery and a dedicated account contact.
What payment methods do you accept for wholesale? +
We accept EFT, credit card via PayFast, Visa, Mastercard, and SnapScan. For standing orders we can also arrange 30-day payment terms for established trade partners.
Can I order a sample before committing? +
Yes! We offer a sample pack for R250 (refundable against your first wholesale order) so you can experience the quality firsthand before placing a bulk order. Call us on 083 299 3949 to arrange.
Do you supply nationwide? +
Yes, we ship nationwide across South Africa. Free delivery within Gauteng on orders over R800. Courier costs for other provinces are calculated at checkout based on your location and order size.
