Our Story

Made by Women,
for Women

Sage & Stitch was born from the same heart as Neshehe Girls — a love for things that are real, handmade, and rooted in nature. Where our soaps carry real petals and fruit, our crochet carries real soul.

Every piece is handcrafted by women in South Africa using natural fibres and earthy palettes inspired by the landscape — terracotta dust roads, sage fynbos, sandy beaches, and golden sunsets.

🌿
Natural Materials
We use cotton, linen, jute, and natural fibres — nothing synthetic.
